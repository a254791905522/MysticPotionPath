Privacy notice

Pave Push Puzzle

This notice describes how the Pave Push Puzzle mobile app may handle information when you play on iPhone or iPad, including data shown on Apple’s App Store privacy label.

📲

Local gameplay

Level progress and settings are stored on your device inside the app sandbox. No account sign-in is required to play.

📢

Advertising

The app may show ads. Ad partners can process identifiers or usage signals to deliver and measure campaigns, subject to your iOS privacy choices.

📈

Usage data

Apple’s listing indicates usage-related data may be collected, linked to you, and used for tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

What Apple’s App Privacy label reports

For the current store listing, usage data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, and usage data may be collected and linked to your identity. Labels can change with updates—review the live App Store page for your installed version.

Scope

This notice applies only to Pave Push Puzzle distributed on the Apple App Store for iOS and iPadOS. It does not cover unrelated websites, personal blogs, or third-party stores.

Categories of information

Depending on the features in your build, the app or its integrated services may process:

  • Gameplay state — Puzzle progress, preferences, and session data kept locally so you can resume sites and levels.
  • Technical and usage signals — Interactions, performance metrics, or diagnostic events to maintain stability and understand how players use stages.
  • Advertising-related data — Device or advertising identifiers and engagement data where ads are shown, within platform limits you set on the device.

Purposes of processing

Information may be processed to run core puzzle gameplay, save progress, display and measure advertising, analyze feature usage, fix crashes, prevent abuse, and meet legal requirements. Exact purposes depend on the SDKs active in each release.

Recipients and retention

Service providers that supply analytics, advertising, or crash reporting may receive data only as needed for those functions. Retention follows each provider’s policies. Removing the app or clearing its data in iOS Settings deletes local sandbox information.

Age rating

The App Store lists a 4+ rating and notes that the app contains advertising. The game is not built around creating accounts for children. Guardians can use Screen Time and system privacy controls to limit tracking and purchases.

Your choices

  • Delete the app or reset its storage in Settings to clear on-device data.
  • Use Privacy & Security and App Tracking Transparency to limit cross-app tracking where available.
  • Check the App Store privacy label after each update for changes to categories or partners.
  • Contact product support through the channel listed on the App Store if you have privacy questions about this title.

Updates to this notice

We may revise this page when gameplay, advertising, or analytics change. If the on-device experience or App Store label differs from this summary, rely on the store listing and your device settings for the version you have installed.

This page is a plain-language summary for players and is not legal advice. No personal contact details or individual developer homepages are listed here. For privacy inquiries about the product, use the support option on the App Store listing for Pave Push Puzzle.
