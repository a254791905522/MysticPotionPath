Pave Push Puzzle

Roll · paint · pave the site

Free iOS 15+ Casual iPhone & iPad

Put on your hard hat and master the grid. Pave Push Puzzle is a construction-themed isometric logic game. Drive a loader across complex sites, roll heavy blocks into place, and paint the right faces before you run out of moves or time. Tight corners, site obstacles, and strict objectives turn every layout into a brain-teasing shift.

🏗️

Grid sites

Navigate low-poly isometric yards filled with trucks, materials, and tricky tile layouts. Plan each route before you commit.

🚜

Loader control

Maneuver your vehicle to push blocks across the board, meet paint objectives, and reach the finish tile to clear the job.

How to play

  1. Survey the site — Study the grid, obstacles, and what each block must look like when the job is done.
  2. Roll and paint — Push blocks along paths so the correct sides get painted while you conserve moves.
  3. Beat the limit — Finish objectives and hit the exit before moves or the clock run out.
  4. Deploy power-ups — Use special tools to escape dead ends, protect progress, and complete tough layouts.
